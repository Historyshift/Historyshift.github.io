Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

History Shift publishes short educational history videos on YouTube, Instagram and TikTok. To do that, the operator of History Shift uses a private tool, the History Shift Uploader, which uploads our own videos to our own accounts through the official APIs of those platforms.

Who the tool is for

The History Shift Uploader is used only by the operator of History Shift, to manage the History Shift accounts. It is not offered to the public and no other person can sign in to it.

YouTube API Services

The History Shift Uploader uses the YouTube API Services to upload History Shift's videos to the History Shift YouTube channel. By using it, the operator agrees to the YouTube Terms of Service. Data obtained through the YouTube API Services is handled according to this policy and to the Google Privacy Policy.

  • What it accesses: permission to upload videos to the History Shift channel, and the channel's name and ID (to confirm the upload goes to the right channel).
  • How it is used: only to upload History Shift's own videos and confirm the channel. It is not sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.
  • Where it is stored: the OAuth refresh token is stored on the operator's computer and as an encrypted secret of a private GitHub repository that runs the daily upload job. No other YouTube data is stored.
  • Revoking access: access can be revoked at any time from the Google security settings page.
  • Deletion: if access is revoked, or on request to the contact address below, the stored token is deleted within 7 days.

What data the tool accesses

  • The access permissions (OAuth tokens) that YouTube, Instagram and TikTok grant to the History Shift accounts. They are stored on the operator's computer and as encrypted secrets of a private GitHub repository used for scheduled uploads, and are never shared.
  • Basic information about the History Shift accounts themselves (account name and ID), used to confirm that videos are uploaded to the right account.

The tool does not access, collect, store or share any data about viewers, followers or any other users.

This website

This website does not use cookies, analytics or tracking of any kind. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may keep technical server logs as described in its own privacy statement.

Third-party platforms

When you watch our videos on YouTube, Instagram or TikTok, those platforms process your data under their own privacy policies. We only see the aggregated statistics those platforms provide to every creator.

Deleting data

Because the tool stores no personal data about anyone other than the History Shift accounts, there is nothing to delete for other users. Access granted to the tool can be revoked at any time from the security settings of each platform.
